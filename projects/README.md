# Sam Bezzant | Selected projects

I build data products around questions I encounter on the water. These two projects approach the Columbia River and the wider Pacific Northwest from different angles.

## Chrome.ai

Can a seven-day estimate of the Chinook catch rate at Buoy 10 improve on a seasonal baseline? I built a fisheries data pipeline, evaluated models on later years, and froze a prospective experiment for August 2027. The case study explains how I handle missing observations, compare methods fairly, and preserve the data available at forecast time.

[Read the case study](chrome-ai.md) · [Visit the research site](https://chrome-ai-research.vercel.app/research)

## RateMyGuidePNW

An angler who knows a guide by a nickname or the name on a boat still needs to find the right person. The public directory lets visitors look up Oregon and Washington fishing guides and open individual profiles. The project preserves source identities so a search alias can help someone find a person without transferring a review to a different guide. Review submission and moderation are in release testing.

[Browse the guide directory](https://ratemyguidepnw.vercel.app/guides) · [View the project repository](https://github.com/bezzantsam/ratemyguidepnw.com)
